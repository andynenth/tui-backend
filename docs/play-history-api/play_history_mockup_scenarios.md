# Play History Mockup Scenarios - Quick Reference

## Mockup 1: Poker-Inspired (High Stakes, Bluffing)
**Theme**: Texas Hold'em style with calculated risks
- **Key Player**: Daniel with GENERAL_RED dominance
- **Strategy Focus**: Minimal declaration (1 pile) with overwhelming power
- **Notable Pattern**: All players played conservatively in Turn 1 (all SOLDIER_RED)
- **Winner**: Daniel (+14 points) through overcapture strategy
- **Lesson**: Sometimes holding the strongest card allows aggressive underdeclaration

- **Conservative** (1 pile): 15% of declarations
- **Standard** (2 piles): 60% of declarations
- **Aggressive** (3 piles): 25% of declarations

## Data Insights for Analysis

### Hand Strength Indicators
- **Instant Win**: GENERAL_RED (9999) or GENERAL_BLACK (10000)
- **High Control**: ADVISOR pairs (120-140 combined)
- **Medium Power**: ELEPHANT/HORSE combinations (80-100)
- **Flexibility**: Multiple small pieces for tactical plays

### Turn Dynamics
- **Opening Moves**: Often minimal (SOLDIER plays)
- **Mid-Game**: Power pieces emerge
- **End-Game**: Forced plays with remaining cards

### Scoring Patterns
- **Exact Match**: 2x multiplier (most common in balanced games)
- **Overcapture**: 1x multiplier (high-risk strategy)
- **Undercapture**: Negative points (failed execution)
